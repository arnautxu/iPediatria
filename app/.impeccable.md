# iPediatria — Design Context

## Project

**iPediatria & iDoctor** — Pediatrics + family medicine clinic on the Costa Brava, established 2010. Home visits + online clinic + in-person consultation at Palafrugell. Primary differentiator: doctor comes to you, no queues, 7am–midnight, 7 days a week. Multi-language CA/EN/RU.

The canonical structural rules (spacing scale, shadow depths, component patterns, pre-delivery checklist) live in `design-system/ipediatria/MASTER.md` (generated by ui-ux-pro-max). **This file overrides MASTER.md for color, typography, motion, and brand voice — because those are already locked by the existing brand and ratified product.**

---

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary**: Anxious parents whose child just got sick — likely at night, on a weekend, or during a holiday on the Costa Brava. They are searching with one hand while holding their kid with the other. They want **certainty within minutes**.

**Secondary**: Tourists with second homes in S'Agaró, Palamós, Begur, Llafranc. They're not from here. They need: "Can someone come, today, who speaks our language?"

**Tertiary**: Long-term local families. Year-round residents who already use iPediatria as their family doctor.

**Emotional state on landing**: stress, urgency, mild distrust of "online" health offerings. **Job to be done**: get reassurance that a real, qualified, warm professional will help — fast, without bureaucracy.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **trustworthy · warm · accessible**.

Voice: factual, calm, plain-spoken Catalan. Never salesy. Never "amazing/incredible/revolutionary." Specific facts > adjectives. Already reworked in `src/i18n/translations.js` (Ogilvy pass). Do not regress the copy.

Emotional goal: a parent should feel *seen* and *relieved* within 3 seconds.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Locked**: cyan #00AEEF + magenta #EC008C duotone. Myriad Pro everywhere. Cream background paper. Editorial confidence with Mediterranean warmth.

**Working references**: Apple Health's clinical-but-warm typography; Linear's restraint with depth; Headspace's color discipline; The Browser Company's confident hero scenes.

**Anti-references** (never look like):
- Generic AI-generated medical landing pages (gradient backgrounds, stock white-coat doctor photo with thumbs up, cyan-on-dark "Get Started" buttons)
- Hospital-website-from-2008 (Times New Roman, table-based layout, multiple stock medical icons in rainbow colors)
- Y Combinator early-stage SaaS template (Inter font, purple gradients, abstract 3D shapes)
- Crypto/fintech dashboard aesthetic (dark mode with neon glow, monospace everywhere)

### Tokens — single source of truth

The CSS tokens at `src/styles/tokens.css` are **canonical**. ui-ux-pro-max's MASTER.md is a guide, not a replacement.

Hard-locked:
```
--cyan:    #00AEEF
--magenta: #EC008C
--cream:   #EAF4FB
--ink:     #414042
--font-display: 'myriad-pro', 'Myriad Pro', ...
--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)
```

Spacing scale (already implicit in clamp() values): roughly 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96. Keep this 4pt-anchored rhythm.

### Design Principles

1. **Specific over general.** "7 a 24 h, 7 dies" beats "available always." A photo of the doctor beats a stock smile.
2. **Confidence through restraint.** When in doubt: less. Editorial whitespace. No gradient text. No border-left stripes. No glassmorphism outside Hero/Nav.
3. **Motion is a tool, not decoration.** Each animation must answer "why?" The Hero scene, Consultori spotlight, Nav morphing pill, mockup float — all earn their place. Don't add new motion without a reason.
4. **Trust is built in the small details.** Hairline shadows, layered highlights, sculpted CTAs that look pressable. Avoid flat colored rectangles for primary actions.
5. **The product is the hero.** Not the design. If a user can do their job (find a phone number, click the consultori CTA, see a real face) without noticing the design, design is winning.

## Anti-AI-slop guardrails — enforce on every commit

Reject any change that introduces:

1. **Border-left accent stripes** on cards, list items, callouts, alerts (any `border-left > 1px` is forbidden).
2. **Gradient text** (`background-clip: text` + gradient). Solid colors only for text.
3. **Glassmorphism** outside Hero featured card and Navigation. Other sections: solid or layered gradient backgrounds.
4. **AI medical icon stock set** (rounded-corner SVG with the same stroke weight on every concept). Each new icon must have a distinct conceptual detail.
5. **Hero metric template** (big number, small label, supporting stats, gradient accent).
6. **Same padding everywhere** — rhythm requires varied spacing.
7. **"Get Started"-style CTAs.** All CTAs name the action: "Accedir al consultori", "Demanar visita a domicili."
8. **Pure black or pure white.** Already enforced: `--ink: #414042`, `--cream: #EAF4FB`. Never `#000` or `#fff` as text/background.

---

## Output guarantee

Every commit must:

- Build without new errors (`npm run build`)
- Maintain contrast ≥4.5:1 for body, ≥3:1 for large text
- Keep all interactive elements ≥44×44px touch target
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (all motion already guarded — don't break this)
- Not regress mobile (375px viewport must scroll cleanly)
- Not introduce a new font dependency (Myriad Pro is the only display+body font)

When in doubt about whether something is "iPediatria enough," ask: *would a stressed parent feel more or less reassured by this?*
